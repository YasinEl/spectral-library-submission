    Required fields and accepted header names

    Each spectrum needs a structure, an adduct that reproduces the precursor m/z, and at least two ions. The other fields can come from the file or be entered in step 2. Ontology fields accept a PSI-MS name, synonym or accession, such as ESI or MS:1000073.
